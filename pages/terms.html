Ads

Sodogku is free to play and pays for itself with ads served by Google AdMob. The only kind of ad in the app is a rewarded one: it is shown when you choose to watch it in exchange for something in the game, such as a refill of bones or covering a missed day of a streak. There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play.

Because the free game depends on ads, it needs an internet connection from time to time. Offline, you can keep playing for a while after the first ad that could not be loaded; after that the app asks you to reconnect, or to buy Sodogku Pro, which plays offline without limit. When an ad cannot be loaded and you are online, the reward is still granted.

Sodogku Pro

Sodogku sells one thing: Sodogku Pro, a single purchase made through Google Play or the App Store at the price the store shows you. It is not a subscription and nothing renews. Pro removes ads, allows unlimited offline play, grants continues, skips and streak freezes without an ad to watch, tops boosters back up at the start of every attempt, and opens every level in the campaign. The purchase screen lists what Pro includes at the time you buy.

The purchase is recorded against your store account, not against your phone. Restore purchases, in Settings, brings it back after a reinstall or on a new device signed in to the same store account. Payment, receipts and refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple under their own terms; we never see your payment details and cannot issue a refund ourselves.

Progress and in-game items

Bones, boosters, streak freezes and anything else the game hands out have no money value, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be transferred. Along with your levels, scores, streaks and badges they live only on your phone. There are no accounts, so uninstalling the app deletes them and we cannot restore them. Sodogku Pro is the exception, because it lives with your store account as described above.
